# Limitations

Rollcall is a hackathon prototype for campus drills and internal safety roll calls. It is not a certified life-safety system.

- The demo uses synthetic roster data and is clearly labeled as Demo Mode.
- A person who does not respond is recorded as `UNACCOUNTED`, never as `SAFE`.
- The system does not infer distress from voice tone.
- Telegram delivery depends on internet access, Telegram availability, and correct chat IDs.
- Outbound phone calls are out of scope for this prototype.
- Production use would require authorized responders, privacy review, failover systems, and jurisdiction-specific emergency procedures.
